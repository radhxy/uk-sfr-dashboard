Definitions

Bulk deal
Five or more new-build houses on one street, registered on the same day to a non-private buyer (Land Registry category B). Likely SFR/PRS when the median price is at least 80% of local open-market new-build prices.
Discount to open market
One minus the deal's median price over the median standard new-build house price in the same council and year, weighted by houses.
Market yield
ONS rent × 12, less operating costs, divided by the Land Registry average price for the chosen house types. ONS rents cover all tenancies, so they trail new-let rents.
Investor yield (net initial yield)
ONS rent raised by the new-let uplift, × 12, less operating costs, divided by the average price less the bulk discount and grossed up for purchaser's costs. All assumptions are set in the Yield basis panel and saved in your browser.
Yield on cost
Net rent divided by total development cost (land, build, fees and finance). The return if you fund the scheme at cost.
Profit on cost
Developer's margin on the bulk sale: exit price less total development cost, over total development cost.
Sales rate
Open-market reservations per sales outlet per week. Housebuilders report it as their main velocity measure.
